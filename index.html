About Dinosaurs

Dinosaurs were a diverse group of reptiles that lived during the Mesozoic Era. They first appeared around 230 million years ago and flourished in environments ranging from lush forests to open plains.

Some were enormous plant-eaters, while others were swift predators. Fossils—preserved bones, footprints, and other traces—help scientists piece together how these remarkable animals lived.

THE END OF AN ERA

The Great Extinction

About 66 million years ago, an asteroid struck the area now known as the Yucatán Peninsula. The impact triggered dramatic environmental changes, leading to the extinction of all non-avian dinosaurs.

But dinosaurs did not disappear completely: birds are the living descendants of a group of feathered theropod dinosaurs.
